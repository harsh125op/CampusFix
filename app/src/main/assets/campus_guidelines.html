Campus Maintenance & Safety Guidelines

CampusFix Policy: CampusFix is designed for offline-first campus issue logging and tracking. All reported issues are assigned a unique Report ID and stored securely on your device.

1. Reporting Guidelines

  • Be Specific with Location: Include building name, floor, room number, or nearest landmark (e.g., Engineering Building 2nd Floor, Room 204).
  • Clear Descriptions: Provide a concise summary of the issue, observed hazards, or equipment malfunctions.
  • Attach Photos: When safe to do so, attach a clear photo of the damaged area or broken facility.

2. Priority Classification

Issues reported via CampusFix are automatically evaluated using our rule-based assessment engine:

  • CRITICAL: Immediate safety hazards, active gas leaks, exposed live wiring, active flooding, or structural collapse.
  • HIGH: Major utility disruptions (AC failure in exam halls, water leaks, lab equipment power outage).
  • MEDIUM: Non-emergency maintenance (flickering lights, broken door locks, elevator noise).
  • LOW: Cosmetic repairs, minor desk/chair damage, general cleaning requests.
EMERGENCY WARNING: If you observe an active fire, medical emergency, or severe danger, do NOT rely solely on app submission. Immediately contact Campus Security (555-0199) or local emergency services (911).

3. Response & Resolution Expectations

  • Critical Issues: Investigated within 1 hour.
  • High Priority: Investigated within 24 hours.
  • Medium Priority: Addressed within 2-3 business days.
  • Low Priority: Scheduled during routine maintenance cycles.
